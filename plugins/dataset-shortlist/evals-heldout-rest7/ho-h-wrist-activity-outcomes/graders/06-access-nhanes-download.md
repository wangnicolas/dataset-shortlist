PASS if the final answer mentions the NHANES accelerometer data and states, or clearly conveys, that the NHANES physical activity monitor files can be downloaded freely from the CDC/NCHS website without registration or an application (if mortality is discussed, the public-use linked mortality files are likewise freely downloadable; adding that restricted linked data need an NCHS Research Data Center application does not cause a fail). FAIL if the answer does not mention NHANES at all, or says or implies that the public NHANES accelerometer files require an application, a data use agreement or a fee.
