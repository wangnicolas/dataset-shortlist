PASS if the final answer does not present the Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS, the CDC's state-based telephone health survey) as a source of measured blood pressure. The user needs blood pressure measured by a nurse or examiner; BRFSS is a telephone survey in which all responses are self-reported, so it has no measured blood pressure. Not mentioning BRFSS passes; mentioning it while making clear that its hypertension information is self-reported only (for example, as a contrast or for self-reported prevalence) passes. FAIL if the answer recommends BRFSS as providing measured blood pressure, or lists it among the surveys that meet the measured blood pressure requirement without noting that its data are self-reported.
