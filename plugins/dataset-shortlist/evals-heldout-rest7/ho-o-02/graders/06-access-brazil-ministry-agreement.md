PASS if the final answer mentions Brazil's RAIS and states that the identified version (with the worker and firm identifiers needed to follow individuals across employers) is restricted and is obtained through a formal request to, and agreement with, the Brazilian Ministry of Labour and Employment (for example a technical cooperation agreement, data-use agreement or similar approval), as opposed to the de-identified public files.
