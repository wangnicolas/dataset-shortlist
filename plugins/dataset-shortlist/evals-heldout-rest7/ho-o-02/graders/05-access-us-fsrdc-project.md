PASS if the final answer mentions the US Census Bureau's LEHD microdata and states that access requires an approved research project or proposal and takes place inside a secure Federal Statistical Research Data Center (FSRDC, also called a Census Research Data Center or RDC), for example also mentioning Special Sworn Status or additional IRS/SSA approval. Stating either the approved-project requirement or the FSRDC-only access is enough. FAIL if the answer does not mention LEHD microdata, gives no information about how they are accessed, or states that the worker-level LEHD microdata can be downloaded publicly.
