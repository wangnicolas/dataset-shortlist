PASS if the final answer states that MDVR-KCL (Mobile Device Voice Recordings at King's College London) can be downloaded openly from Zenodo without an application or approval. If the answer names its licence, the licence must be Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). FAIL if the answer does not mention MDVR-KCL, says it must be requested or approved before download, or gives a different licence (for example a non-commercial one).
