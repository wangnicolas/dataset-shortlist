PASS if the final answer states that the Low Carbon London smart meter data ("SmartMeter Energy Consumption Data in London Households", LCL) can be downloaded directly, without an application, from the London Datastore. If the answer names its licence, the licence must be Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY, version 4.0 on the London Datastore page). FAIL if the answer does not mention this dataset, says it must be applied for, or gives a different licence (for example a non-commercial one).
