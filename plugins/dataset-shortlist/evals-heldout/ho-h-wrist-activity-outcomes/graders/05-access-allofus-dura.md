PASS if the final answer mentions the All of Us Research Program data and states, or clearly conveys, that access to its individual-level data (including the Fitbit data) is through the All of Us Researcher Workbench and requires that the researcher's institution has signed a Data Use and Registration Agreement (DURA) with the program, in addition to the researcher registering and completing the required training. Wording such as "your institution must have an agreement with All of Us" passes. FAIL if the answer does not mention All of Us at all, or says or implies that the data can be downloaded freely, or describes access without any indication that an institutional agreement is required.
