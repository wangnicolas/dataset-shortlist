PASS if the final answer recommends or lists the confidential microdata of the US Census Bureau's Longitudinal Employer-Household Dynamics program (LEHD, also called the LEHD microdata, LEHD infrastructure files or LEHD Snapshot, built from state unemployment-insurance wage records) as a candidate source of linked employer-employee data. FAIL if the answer does not mention LEHD microdata, mentions only the public LEHD tabulations (QWI, LODES, J2J or OnTheMap) without the restricted worker-level microdata, or mentions LEHD only to say it is unsuitable.
