PASS if the final answer mentions CHARLS (China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study) and states, or clearly conveys, that obtaining the data requires registering an account on the CHARLS website (signing its user agreement and giving real personal information) and submitting a data application that the CHARLS team reviews and approves before download. Wording such as "free, but you must register and your request is approved within a few days" passes. FAIL if the answer does not mention CHARLS at all, or says or implies that CHARLS data can be downloaded anonymously with no registration or approval, or gives no indication of how CHARLS data are obtained.
