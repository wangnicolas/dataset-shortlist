PASS if the final answer mentions the Health Survey for England (HSE) and states, or clearly conveys, that its microdata are obtained through the UK Data Service and require registering with the UK Data Service and accepting its End User Licence (they are "safeguarded" data, for non-commercial use), rather than being an anonymous direct download. Wording such as "free from the UK Data Service after you register and agree to the licence" passes, and mentioning further conditions (for example non-commercial use only, or limits on the countries where the data may be stored and accessed) is correct and does not cause a fail. FAIL if the answer does not mention the HSE at all, or says or implies that HSE microdata can be downloaded with no registration or licence agreement, or gives no indication of where or how the HSE data are obtained.
