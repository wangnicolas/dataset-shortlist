PASS if the final answer mentions NHANES and states, or clearly conveys, that its public-use data files (including the blood pressure examination and questionnaire files) can be downloaded directly and free of charge from the CDC/NCHS NHANES website without registration, an application or a data use agreement. Adding that some restricted variables (for example detailed geography) require a Research Data Center application is correct and does not cause a fail. FAIL if the answer does not mention NHANES at all, or says or implies that the public NHANES files require registration, an application, an agreement or a fee.
